I'm Zisan Mahmud. I finished my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Dhaka in 2026 with a CGPA of 3.75 out of 4.00, and I'm now doing my M.Sc. in the same department. My research interests are AI, NLP, data mining, educational data mining and graph mining.

My thesis was on Bangla memes: how a meme carries a cultural metaphor, and how a model can interpret it. With my supervisor Md. Fahim Arefin, I built BanglaMemeX, a multimodal explainable dataset for this task, and the paper was accepted to Findings of EMNLP 2026. I also co-authored a TMLR 2026 paper on hyperedge anomaly detection with hypergraph neural networks.

I teach Computer Algorithms, both theory and lab, as an Adjunct Lecturer at International Islamic University Chittagong. Next, I want to focus on applying AI in education and on finding ways to get more benefit out of educational AI.
